## Job 1 - Cybersecurity Engineer - Junior

**Company:** Leonardo Belgium

### Responsibilities

- Contribute to the deployment and operation of cybersecurity solutions and controls.
- Support the secure configuration and hardening of systems, applications and network components.
- Assist with vulnerability remediation and security improvement activities.
- Participate in troubleshooting and resolving security-related issues.
- Help integrate security into infrastructure and development environments.
- Maintain technical documentation, procedures and implementation records.
- Collaborate with cybersecurity architects, risk managers and other security stakeholders.
- Support reporting activities and communicate progress to project teams.

### Education and experience

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Information Technology or a related field, or an alternative educational path supported by proven experience.
- Initial professional experience in cybersecurity, with exposure to implementing or operating security solutions.

### Expected skills and qualities

- Structured and analytical mindset.
- Strong problem-solving skills.
- Curiosity and willingness to learn.
- Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced international environment.
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with fluent English.

### Desired technical exposure

- Networks, operating systems and fundamental security technologies.
- Cybersecurity principles, standards and best practices.
- Security hardening and vulnerability management.
- Security monitoring, incident detection or security operations.
- General interest in cybersecurity and the changing threat landscape.

### Nice to have

- Hands-on experience with security technologies or infrastructure environments.
- Exposure to security monitoring tools, SIEM platforms or SOC environments.
- Knowledge of cloud technologies and cloud security concepts.
- Scripting and automation skills such as Python, PowerShell or Ansible.
- Cybersecurity certifications.
- Exposure to EU institutions, NATO or other regulated environments.
- Exposure to emerging technologies such as AI and quantum technologies.
- French and/or Italian language skills.

## Job 2 - Young Graduates CyberSecurity

**Company:** Devoteam Belgium

### Mission

- Join a team of junior security consultants delivering "Solutions as a Service".
- Support clients' digital transformation through three pillars: Cyber Resilience, Applied Security and Managed Services, enhanced by Artificial Intelligence.

### Focus 1 - Applied Security and AI Innovation

- Apply AI and machine learning to automate threat detection and provide intelligent access.
- Secure multi-cloud workloads through Cloud Security Posture Management and Cloud Workload Protection.
- Implement identity and access solutions such as PAM, IAM and Identity Governance.
- Support the integration of container security into modern DevSecOps lifecycles.

### Focus 2 - Cyber Resilience

- Participate in proactive security activities such as red teaming, web and API penetration testing, and social engineering simulations.
- Support AI security governance, including the management of Shadow AI and LLM vulnerabilities.
- Contribute to cyber maturity roadmaps, including Zero Trust, and technology selection.
- Support security governance, risk and compliance activities involving NIS/NIS2, ISO 27K and NIST 800-53.

### Focus 3 - Managed Services

- Help solve cybersecurity challenges through the "Solutions as a Service" model.
- Support managed services with AI-powered insights to anticipate risks.

### Candidate profile

- Degree in Cybersecurity, Computer Science or a related technical field.
- Strong interest in the impact of AI on threats and its defensive applications.
- Continuous-improvement mindset.
- Ability to turn cyber risk management and threat modelling into actionable insights for stakeholders.

## Job 3 - Junior Security Engineer

**Company:** Sopra Steria Belgium

### Profile

- Degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience.
- Good understanding of security concepts.
- Open-minded, with good communication skills.
- Analytical mindset and problem-solving ability.
- French or Dutch together with English.
- Another EU language or study/work experience elsewhere in the EU is an advantage.

### Technical experience and skills

Candidates should have at least one of the following areas of experience:

- Network security weaknesses and mitigations at protocol and equipment level, including Cisco, Juniper and OpenVPN.
- Windows Domain security assessment and mitigations, including Domain Controllers and Active Directory.
- Cryptography and PKI, including EJBCA, OpenSSL, PKCS#11, OpenTrust and Microsoft CA/PKI.
- Linux hardening and hardening techniques for operating system distributions.
- macOS and iOS security models.
- Cryptographic libraries such as Bouncy Castle.
- SSO protocols and systems such as SAML, OAuth2 and Gluu.
- Two-factor authentication protocols and tools such as FIDO U2F.
- Security tools such as Nmap, Nessus, Kali, Suricata, Wireshark, Tcpdump, Metasploit, Nexpose, IDA Pro, OSSEC, Elasticsearch, Splunk, OpenSSH and sudo.
- OWASP Top 10 exploitation and defense techniques, including XSS and CSRF.
- Virtual machine security analysis and configuration, including VMware, Xen and Docker.
- Digital forensics and security analytics.
- Deep learning applied to behavioral security.
- Cryptographic tools such as PGP and hardware security modules.
- Threat information exchange, for example MISP.
- Mobile security and trusted execution environments.
- Security code review using Coverity, Fortify or open-source alternatives.
- Risk management tools such as EBIOS, CRAMM or Verinice.

### Additional advantages

- Ability to explain a mitigation clearly, from its core principle to its technical details.
- Attendance or presentations at security conferences such as FOSDEM Security Devroom or Brucon.
- Scripting knowledge, including Python, PowerShell, Perl or Ruby.
- SANS GAWP certification or an equivalent certification.
- Participation in hacking challenges or open-source security projects.

### Career opportunity

The role offers hands-on experience, training and the possibility of developing toward senior security engineering, cybersecurity or PKI architecture, digital forensics or other specialist roles.

## Common priorities

The counts below are based on the three job descriptions reviewed. They indicate recurring themes, not mandatory requirements for every position.

### Priority 1 - Mentioned in all three jobs

- `3x` - Education or equivalent experience in cybersecurity, computer science or IT.
- `3x` - Solid understanding of security concepts and principles.
- `3x` - Communication skills with teams and stakeholders.
- `3x` - Curiosity, willingness to learn and continuous improvement.

### Priority 2 - Mentioned in two jobs

- `2x` - Analytical thinking and problem-solving.
- `2x` - Hardening, vulnerability mitigation and security posture improvement.
- `2x` - Security operations, monitoring or analytics, including SIEM, SOC and detection.
- `2x` - Identity and access management, including IAM, SSO and 2FA.
- `2x` - Scripting and automation, especially Python or PowerShell.
- `2x` - Exposure to international or EU environments and multilingual communication.
- `2x` - Cybersecurity certifications as an advantage.

### Priority 3 - Mentioned in one job

- `1x` - Formal governance, risk and compliance, including NIS/NIS2, ISO 27K and NIST 800-53.
- `1x` - Offensive security, including red teaming, web/API penetration testing and social engineering.
- `1x` - AI security governance, Shadow AI, LLM vulnerabilities and Zero Trust roadmaps.
- `1x` - Explicit exposure to quantum technologies.

## Practical takeaways

For a junior cybersecurity profile in Belgium, the most useful foundations are:

1. Networking, operating systems and core security principles.
2. Linux and Windows hardening, vulnerability management and basic incident handling.
3. One practical security track, such as SOC/SIEM, IAM, cloud security, penetration testing or PKI.
4. Python or PowerShell automation.
5. Clear technical communication in English, plus French or Dutch for the Belgian market.
6. A small portfolio of labs, security projects, write-ups or certifications demonstrating hands-on practice.
